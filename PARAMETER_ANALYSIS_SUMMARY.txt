CRITICAL PARAMETERS FOR PARAMETER COUNT CONTROL
================================================================================

TRANSFORMER FAMILY (Most Important):
1. d_model (embedding dimension)
   - Effect: O(d_model²) on attention params
   - Typical values: 64, 128, 256, 512
   - Recommendation: Standardize to 128

2. num_layers / e_layers
   - Effect: Linear on total params
   - Typical values: 2, 3, 4, 8
   - Recommendation: Use 3-4 for Tier 1

3. dim_feedforward / d_ff
   - Effect: O(d_model × dim_ff) per layer
   - Typical values: 256, 512, 1024, 2048
   - Recommendation: Keep to 256-512 for Tier 1

RNN FAMILY:
1. hidden_size
   - Effect: O(hidden_size²) on recurrent params
   - Typical values: 128, 256
   - Recommendation: Standardize to 128-256

2. num_layers
   - Effect: Linear on total params
   - Typical values: 2, 3, 4
   - Recommendation: Use 2-3 for Tier 1

CONVOLUTIONAL FAMILY:
1. num_channels (list of channel widths per layer)
   - Effect: Sum of conv filters
   - Typical values: [64,128,128,256], [128,256,256,512]
   - Recommendation: Control via width multiplier

2. num_blocks
   - Effect: Linear on total params (ModernTCN)
   - Typical values: 6-12
   - Recommendation: Adjust based on target

MAMBA/STATE-SPACE:
1. embed_dim (token embedding)
   - Effect: O(embed_dim²) on attention-like operations
   - Typical values: 128, 256, 512
   - Recommendation: Standardize to 128 (MambaTSA) / 256 (Mamba2 reduced)

2. state_dim (SSM state size)
   - Effect: O(embed_dim × state_dim) on scanning
   - Typical values: 16, 32, 64, 128, 256
   - Recommendation: Keep proportional to embed_dim (~0.5x)
